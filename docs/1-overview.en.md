**Make Odoo the beating heart of your business** :rocket:

Your ERP should not live on an island. With the Splash Sync module for Odoo, your customers, products, stocks,
orders and invoices flow automatically between Odoo and all the applications you already use:
e-commerce platforms, marketplaces, CRM, logistics or accounting tools.

No more copy-paste, no more CSV imports, no more stock errors: enter your data once, Splash takes care of the rest.

### Why Splash for Odoo?

- :zap: **Real-time synchronization**: every change made in Odoo is pushed to your connected apps, and vice versa.
- :arrows_counterclockwise: **Two-way, field by field**: you decide which application is the reference for each piece of data.
- :jigsaw: **No-code mapping**: link Odoo fields to your other apps from your Splash workspace, without a single line of code.
- :shield: **Reliable & traceable**: every operation is logged, errors are reported so nothing gets lost silently.
- :package: **Open source**: the module is free and published on GitHub, compatible with Odoo 12.0 up to 18.0.

### What can you synchronize?

| Object | What is shared |
|---|---|
| :bust_in_silhouette: **Customers** | companies & individuals, contact details, languages |
| :house: **Addresses** | billing & delivery addresses linked to your customers |
| :shopping_cart: **Products** | variants & attributes, multilingual descriptions, prices, images, stocks, suppliers |
| :receipt: **Orders** | order lines, taxes, carriers, deliveries & statuses |
| :page_facing_up: **Invoices** | invoice lines, payments & statuses |

### Typical use cases

- Publish your Odoo catalog on your online store and marketplaces, with stocks always up to date.
- Import web orders directly into Odoo, ready to be shipped and invoiced.
- Share a single customer database between Odoo, your shop and your CRM.
- Feed your accounting with invoices and payments, without manual entry.
